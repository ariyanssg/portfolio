## The problem

Making good flashcards takes longer than studying them. Most people stop at highlighting their notes, and highlighting is not practice.

## What I built

A web app that turns study material into a deck you can practise right away.

- **Bring notes in any form.** Paste text, upload a Word or plain-text file, or take a photo of handwritten notes. The app reads the file in the browser and sends it to Google's Gemini model, which writes question-and-answer cards from it.
- **Study four ways.** Classic flip cards, a quiz, a written-answer mode and a matching game.
- **Spaced repetition.** Rating a card easy, medium or hard adjusts its ease factor and schedules the next review, so difficult cards come back sooner.
- **See progress.** Study stats and charts show what has been reviewed and what is due.
- **Make it yours.** Deck templates and a theme customiser.

## How it's built

- React and TypeScript on Vite, with Tailwind CSS and Radix primitives for accessible dialogs, menus and toasts.
- Zustand holds decks and study state.
- Mammoth reads Word files in the browser; images go to Gemini as they are. React Dropzone handles uploads.
- Recharts draws the study statistics.

## Stack

React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Zustand, the Gemini API, Mammoth and Recharts.
